# One-question intake protocol

The interview is deliberately paced like GrillMe: ask one question, wait for
the answer, summarize it, then ask the next. Do not display the full checklist
at once. If the repository or input files already contain a safe answer,
propose it and ask for confirmation instead of guessing.

Ask in this order:

1. Owner alias or `unknown`.
2. Subject alias or `unknown`.
3. Relationship label or `unknown`.
4. Relationship start/end or life stage, or `unknown`.
5. Owner school/organization, student/work status, and role, or skip.
6. Subject school/organization, student/work status, and role, or skip.
7. Shared experiences.
8. Relationship milestones and turning points.
9. Important stories and inside jokes.
10. Subject expression habits the owner wants preserved.
11. Privacy boundary and intended output use.

Accepted non-answers are `unknown`, `不想提供`, and `跳过`. Store them as
`Unknown` without follow-up pressure. Before writing the generated skill,
show a concise draft containing the source coverage, known gaps, and intended
simulation boundary. Write only after explicit confirmation.
